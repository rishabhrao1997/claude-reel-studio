# Reel idea scout (starter)

## 1. Look at what's working
Pick 3–5 creators in your niche. For each, open their reels tab and note the last month's top 2 reels and 1 flop. Score each by views ÷ followers: a reel at 5× the follower count found new people. For every hit, write one line on why it beat the flop (topic, hook, a result you can see).

## 2. The who-cares test
Before writing anything, fill in three lines. If one is weak, drop the idea.
- After watching, the viewer can: ___
- They'd send it to: ___ because: ___
- What's on screen as proof: ___

## 3. Hooks that work
The first line names the result and holds back the method.
- "If you use [tool], here are 3 [things] you need."
- "You can now [surprising thing] in [short time]."
- "What happens when you ask [AI] to [bold task]?"
- "If you [do the normal thing], stop."
- "I didn't [do X] in this video. [AI] did."

## 4. Write the script
- 65–150 words (about 20–45 seconds). Short lines, plain words.
- The viewer is the hero: "you can", not "I did".
- Something real on screen in the first 2 seconds.
- End with one comment keyword tied to something useful you'll send.

Output a table: Time | You say | On screen.
